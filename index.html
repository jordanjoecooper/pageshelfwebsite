A simple reading ritual

From open tab to a page of your own.

The internet is full of things worth reading. PageShelf gives them a calmer place to wait for you.

  1. 01

    Save the good ones

    Add a link in PageShelf or save from Safari. A clean reading copy joins your searchable shelf.

  2. 02

    Read your way

    Choose the type, size, spacing, and appearance that feel comfortable, then pick up where you left off.

  3. 03

    Keep what matters

    Highlight a passage, save a quotation, or add a note. Your ideas stay beside the page.
