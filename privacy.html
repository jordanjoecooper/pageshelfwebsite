Your reading is personal

Privacy, in plain words.

PageShelf is designed to keep your reading library under your control. This policy explains what happens when you use the PageShelf app and website.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who is responsible

PageShelf is made by Jordan Joe Cooper in the United Kingdom. For privacy questions, use the contact options at jordanjoecooper.com.

Your library and notes

The app stores saved article copies, reading progress, favourites, categories, highlights, quotations, notes, and settings on your device. The developer does not receive or store this library on a PageShelf server. You do not need a PageShelf account.

You can export your library or individual pages. Those files are saved or shared only when you choose a destination or recipient.

Article links and website providers

When you ask PageShelf to save a link, your device requests that page from its website so the app can make a reading copy. That website may receive connection information, such as your IP address, and may apply its own privacy policy. If you choose to save article images, your device may also request those images from their hosts.

The PageShelf website does not require an account and is built without advertising or analytics trackers. Its hosting provider may process basic request information to deliver and protect the site.

Optional iCloud Drive backups

If you enable automatic backups, PageShelf writes a separate backup file to your iCloud Drive PageShelf folder. Apple handles storage and synchronisation of that file under your Apple Account and Apple’s privacy terms. The developer does not have access to your iCloud account. This is a backup, not live library sync between devices.

You can turn automatic backups off in PageShelf Settings and manage or remove the backup file from iCloud Drive.

Purchases

PageShelf Full is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Apple processes purchases and restores through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple’s handling is described in Apple’s privacy policy.

Information we collect

The app includes no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. The developer does not operate a PageShelf account service or receive your saved reading content. Apple and the websites you visit may process information as needed to provide their services.

Children

PageShelf is a general reading utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, visit jordanjoecooper.com.
